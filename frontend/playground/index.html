📚Resources & Further Reading

Want to go deeper? Here are the best free resources to continue learning.

3Blue1Brown — Neural Networks

The best visual explanation of neural networks on YouTube. Beautiful animations.

The Illustrated Transformer

Jay Alammar's legendary visual guide to how transformers work step by step.

Karpathy — Neural Networks: Zero to Hero

Build GPT from scratch. The best free course for understanding LLMs deeply.

Hugging Face NLP Course

Free hands-on course covering transformers, fine-tuning, and NLP with code.

DeepLearning.AI Short Courses

Free 1-hour courses on RAG, LangChain, fine-tuning, agents, and more by Andrew Ng.

Attention Is All You Need (Paper)

The 2017 paper that started it all. The original Transformer architecture.

Lilian Weng's Blog

In-depth posts on diffusion models, RLHF, RAG, agents, and more. OpenAI researcher.

fast.ai — Practical Deep Learning

Free course that teaches deep learning top-down. Build things first, theory later.

LLM Course (GitHub)

Complete roadmap to learn LLMs from scratch — fundamentals to production deployment.
